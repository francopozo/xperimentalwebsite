### 2.2 Experiencias listadas

1. **"The Art of the City: Design & Architecture tour in Copenhagen"**
   - Tipo: Insightful Guided Tour
   - Duración: 3h
   - Precio: 3,500 DKK
   - Descripción: Walking tour explorando el centro de Copenhague donde diseño y arquitectura se encuentran. Estilo minimalista, edificios históricos y diseño moderno. Mobiliario danés, estética interior, shops de craftsmanship.

2. **"Arne Jacobsen: Shaping Danish Design and Architecture"**
   - Tipo: Insightful Guided Tour
   - Duración: 2.5h
   - Precio: 2,700 DKK
   - Descripción: Legado de Arne Jacobsen, Ant Chair, SAS Royal Hotel. Recorrido por el centro histórico: Church of Our Lady, City Hall, Tivoli.

3. **Danish Architecture Center (DAC)**
   - Tipo: Insightful Guided Tour
   - Duración: 2h
   - Precio: 3,100 DKK
   - Descripción: Exposiciones en el DAC, edificios daneses icónicos, urbanismo sostenible.

4. **Art and Architecture at the Ny Carlsberg Glyptotek**
   - Tipo: Insightful Guided Tour
   - Duración: 2h
   - Precio: 3,100 DKK
   - Descripción: Museo fundado por Carl Jacobsen (hijo del fundador de Carlsberg). Pinturas impresionistas francesas, Danish Golden Age, Winter Garden. Obras de Van Gogh, Gauguin, Renoir.

5. **Copenhagen architecture tour: Walking the new Copenhagen — The Nordhavn Evolution**
   - Tipo: Insightful Guided Tour
   - Duración: 2.5h
   - Precio: 3,100 DKK
   - Descripción: Nordhavn, distrito de vanguardia donde las raíces industriales se fusionan con arquitectura moderna. The Silo, Portland Towers. Desarrollo urbano sostenible.

6. **"Secret Places" Walkshop with Marie-Louise Høstbo**
   - Tipo: Curated Cultural Activity
   - Duración: 2h
   - Precio: 4,700 DKK (1-10 personas). Suplemento 400 DKK/persona extra. Máx 15.
   - Descripción: Colaboración con Marie-Louise Høstbo (ex Creative Design Director de Fritz Hansen). Autora de "Secret Places – The Architect's Guide to Distinctive Buildings in and around Copenhagen". Recorrido por Christianshavn, Knippelsbro, Slotsholmen, Nytorv, Gammeltorv, City Hall Square. Opción de charla/lecture para grupos y empresas.

## 3. INFO PAGE — Pricing, FAQ, Gift Cards

### 3.1 Pricing & Guidelines

```
At Kultiveret, we design private experiences as
complete cultural journeys through Copenhagen and
beyond, exploring art, design, history, and local life.

Experiences are created for individuals, couples,
families, and small groups, with careful preparation,
aesthetic sensibility, and special access.

Pricing is set for 1–6 guests and noted alongside
each experience. For groups of 7–12, we add DKK 350
per extra guest. If you're traveling with a larger
group, please get in touch.

Most unfold over 2 to 4 hours, allowing time for
immersion, discovery, and thoughtful exploration
at a natural, unhurried pace.
```

### 3.2 FAQ Completo

**Experience details:**
- **Are the experiences private or group-based?** — All our experiences are private, but pre-formed groups are also welcome! Let us know your group size, and we'll create a custom experience for you.
- **Where do your experiences take place?** — Our experiences are based in Copenhagen, but we also offer tours and activities in the surrounding areas, including the beautiful region of Zealand. Let us know your preferences, and we'll tailor the experience to suit you.
- **What languages are the experiences available in?** — We offer experiences in English.

**Payment & Booking:**
- **How do I book?** — To request your curated cultural activities, please fill in the form at any of the activities' description. If you wish to book for a specific date and time, please email us at contact@kultiveret.com. Advance booking is essential to ensure a personalized experience. For any questions or assistance with the booking process, feel free to reach out—we're happy to help.
- **What payment methods do you accept?** — We accept payments via bank transfer (with a 50 DKK fee) or TransferWise. Please include your name and activity details when making the payment.
- **Do I need to pay in advance?** — Yes, full payment is required to confirm your booking.

**Cancellations and Rescheduling:**
- **Can I cancel or reschedule my booking?** — Yes, you can cancel or reschedule if you notify us at least 5 working days before the activity. Cancellations after this will incur a 20% fee, and no-shows or last-minute cancellations are non-refundable.

**Customization and Accessibility:**
- **Can the experiences be customized?** — Yes, all our experiences can be tailored to your interests, whether it's art, design, history, or photography.
- **Are your experiences accessible?** — We do our best to accommodate all guests. Let us know your needs in advance so we can plan accordingly.

**Miscellaneous:**
- **Do you offer gift cards?** — Yes, we offer gift certificates for our tours and experiences. Just ask us for details.
- **Can you recommend restaurants or hotels?** — Absolutely! We'd be happy to suggest options based on your preferences.

## 5. Tono y estilo editorial

### Rasgos del copy
- Oraciones largas pero cuidadosamente medidas, con ritmo pausado.
- Adjetivación contenida y precisa: *immersive, curated, thoughtful, personal, meaningful, unhurried, authentic*.
- Sin exclamaciones, sin urgencia comercial.
- Voz en primera persona del plural ("we design", "we strive", "we collaborate").
- Uso ocasional de primera persona singular para la founder ("I believe").
- Frecuente uso de "—" (em dash) para pausas reflexivas.

### Tratamiento de precios
- Precios en DKK, sin símbolo de moneda en el display principal.
- Rangos de precio: 2,700–4,700 DKK.
- Suplemento por persona extra transparente (+350 DKK).

### UX patterns
- Split-text reveal animations (word-by-word) al hacer scroll.
- Navegación por categorías con imágenes a full-bleed y números (#).
- FAQ con tabs horizontales (no accordion).
- Formulario multi-step (1/2).
- Tooltip en horario de contacto.
- Botón "toggle sound" para experiencia de audio ambiental (Copenhagen sounds).
- CTA principal: "design your visit" (no "book now" o "contact us").

### Elementos interactivos
- Sound toggle (ON/OFF) con wave icon.
- Menú hamburguesa con overlay full-screen.
- Hover states sutiles en tarjetas de categoría.
- Scroll-triggered text reveals.
